# Research liaison: this week

**Your job:** connect Builders and Research. Make sure we only collect what Research trusts, and that we build what they actually need. No coding required.

**Goal by around Oct 20:** Research has approved the new sources and has started using the Reader for their two papers.

## Tasks

### 1. Source approvals
Curators will open a **New source** issue for each source. Take them to Research, and post Research's yes or no as a comment on each issue. Nothing gets `approved: true` without that comment.

**Done when:** every new source issue has Research's answer on it.

### 2. Settle the credibility question
Ask Research to decide:
- Do university papers and preprints (research that isn't final yet) count?
- Are industry groups OK, or only government and academic sources?

Post their answer in Slack for Curators.

### 3. Get Research's top questions
For each paper (energy and data center compute, and privacy and digital identity in healthcare), ask Research for the 5 questions they most want the archive to help answer. Post them in Slack. Curators use them to pick sources, and Reader uses them to design search.

### 4. Test the site with Research
Once the site has real sources, sit with 2 or 3 researchers while they use it. Write down where they get stuck, what they look for and can't find, and what they like. Turn each problem into a GitHub issue that says what's wrong and what "done" looks like.

## How to write a good issue
- **Title:** what's wrong, in a few words ("Can't filter by date")
- **What happened:** what the researcher tried and what they saw
- **Done when:** how we'll know it's fixed ("I can show only items from the last 30 days")

Open the live site, research-pipeline-zeta.vercel.app, and try to use it the way a researcher would.
